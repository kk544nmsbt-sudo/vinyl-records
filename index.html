Rich's Vinyl Records

Every record on the shelf, with its current value, plus the hi-fi that plays them.

    Records are grouped by artist, then by original release year, with compilations and live albums after studio albums. Values for records are the Discogs median sold price, checked 24 November 2026, except three titles with no sales history. Two of those use Amazon UK's current new price, checked 24 November 2026; Radiohead's The King Of Limbs keeps its previous Discogs listing price. Hi-fi values are typical eBay UK sold prices (pre-owned median), checked 24 November 2026. The turntable value is for the Pro-Ject Debut Carbon (the Carbon EVO is unconfirmed). The accessories figure is an approximate value, based on the Pro-Ject Brush it. Tap a cover to see it on Discogs.
